# Design

Numa's design should make intelligence feel calm, useful, and inspectable.

The system should help users understand what it knows, why it surfaced something, and how a recommendation connects to their goals, memory, or knowledge graph.

## Experience Goals

- Make discovery feel intentional rather than noisy.
- Show source provenance and confidence clearly.
- Let users correct memory and assumptions easily.
- Support daily use without demanding constant attention.
- Keep generated insight connected to user-owned context.

## Core Surfaces

### Daily Intelligence Report

A concise report that highlights relevant discoveries, changes, risks, and opportunities.

### Memory Review

A place for users to inspect, correct, merge, or delete remembered facts and preferences.

### Knowledge Graph

A navigable representation of people, topics, sources, projects, and relationships.

### Research Workspace

A focused environment for autonomous research tasks, evidence collection, and synthesis.

## Interaction Principles

- Always make it clear when information is remembered, inferred, or externally sourced.
- Prefer actionable summaries with links to evidence.
- Ask for confirmation before making durable memory changes.
- Surface uncertainty instead of hiding it.
- Keep the user in control of scope, cadence, and sources.
