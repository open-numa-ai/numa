# Vision

Numa exists to help humans discover knowledge beyond their awareness.

The internet made information abundant, but abundance did not solve the deeper discovery problem: people often do not know what questions to ask, what sources to trust, or which assumptions are limiting their view.

Numa aims to become a personal intelligence layer that continuously learns with the user, discovers valuable knowledge, challenges assumptions, and supports long-term growth.

## What Numa Is

Numa is a personal intelligence system built around:

- Long-term memory
- Knowledge discovery
- Personal knowledge graphs
- Autonomous research
- Reasoning and reflection

## What Numa Is Not

Numa is not just another chatbot, feed, bookmark manager, or note-taking app.

It should not simply wait for prompts. It should help users notice what matters, connect ideas over time, and make better decisions with clearer context.

## Product Principles

- Respect the user's agency.
- Make reasoning visible.
- Prefer durable knowledge over disposable output.
- Connect new information to the user's existing context.
- Treat privacy and data ownership as core product requirements.
